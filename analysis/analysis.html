Review Time

How long the add-ons of each week have waited, in days: the median (solid) and the mean (dashed). Every add-on that was in the queue at any time of the week counts, with its age when it was reviewed, or at the end of Sunday if it is still waiting (so those are at least this old). A week in which nothing is reviewed is not empty: it shows how old the waiting add-ons are, and the line rises. Same rule as Review Queue Age.

Median and mean: the median is the middle value, half of the add-ons waited less and half longer, so it is the typical wait and a few extreme ones do not move it. The mean is all the waits added up and divided by their number, so every long wait pulls it up. When the mean is far above the median, a few add-ons waited much longer than the rest.

Review Queue Age

How long submissions have waited. A column is a week, a row a week of age (36 rows, the last one is older), and the labels sit on the edges between the rows. Every submission that was in the queue at any time of a week counts once for it, with its age when it was reviewed, or at the end of Sunday if it was still waiting. So a tower is a stack of blocks and one block is one submission. The colour is the age, see the legend. Drag to turn, hold Ctrl (Cmd on a Mac) to move by dragging and to zoom with the wheel, or use the buttons. Double click resets.
